Here are some photos taken during this business trip. I was able to make the best of the dawn hours, visiting:
- Seaside of Miraflores
- Swamps known as Pantanos de Villa, 20 minutes south of Miraflores
Includes 57 photos covering 26 speices (8 of which are not found in Argentina).
I was also able to get my first photos of 6 species that range into Argentina.
