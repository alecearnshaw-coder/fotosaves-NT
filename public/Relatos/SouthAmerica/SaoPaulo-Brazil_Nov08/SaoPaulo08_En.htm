Here is a small collection of photos taken during a business trip to Sao Paulo.
Luckily I was able to get out briefly to wildlife areas.

Places visited:
- Ibirapuera park: 28/10 early morning
- Parque Burle Marx: evening of 28/10 and early morning on 29/10
- Serra de Cantareira: arrived midnight 30/10 and left 2 pm on 1st November

This page has 35 photos including 14 bird species (10 of which are also found in Argentina).
This trip allowed me to obtain my first photos of 6 species present in Argentina.

Thanks for visiting ths page - Alec Earnshaw
