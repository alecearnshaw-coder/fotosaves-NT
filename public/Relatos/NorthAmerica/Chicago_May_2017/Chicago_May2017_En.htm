Vist to Mt. Prospect, Chicago, IL, USA
14th-21st May, 2017
Copyright (C) 2017 - Alec Earnshaw

Here is a collection of photos taken during a business (training) trip to Chicago.
I was lucky to have a free Sunday before the work week, and a again on Saturday after the training, as well as two quick mid-week visits to the Des Plains river near Mt. Prospect, and even some wildlife to see on the grounds around the company I was visiting.

I wish to especially thank Nan Buckardt, and her son Lucas for having kindly showed me around many of the beautiful nature preserves in Lake County on Sunday 14th.

The places I visited are:

Sunday 14:
- Edward L. Rierson Conservation Area, run by Lake County Forest Preserves
- Middlefork Preserve
- Middlefork Savannah, run by Lake County Forest Preserves
- Fort Sheridan, run by Lake County Forest Preserves

Tuesday 16th:
- Des Plaines river near Camp Pine, in Mt. Prospect.

Thursday 18th:
- Des Plaines river near Camp Pine, in Mt. Prospect, as well as Cummins-Allison Corp. HQalso in Mt. Prospect

Saturday 20th:
- Busse Forest / Ned Brown preserves

Below is a selection of the best photos I took during this trip. This page has 95 photos including:
- 55 species of birds
- 3 species of mammals
- 3 species of reptiles
- 1 species of amphibian
- 1 species of butterfly

Thanks for visiting this page - Alec Earnshaw
