Vist to Panama
22nd May, 2017
Copyright (C) 2017-2024 - Alec Earnshaw

Here is a collection of photos taken during an unexpected layover in Panama, as a result of a flight delay that resulted in missing the connecting flight back to Buenos Aires.
This gave me a full morning to return to the wonderful Parque Municipal Metropolitano, whichI had visited in a rush back in 2005.

Not being familiar with the bird calls, I was very limited at locating and identifying birds. Due to the late arrival I had not been able to arrange a guide, which would have vastly improved the outcome, considering it's a site where 400 species have been recorded. But then, it was more fun to locate by myself the things I did see.

Below is a selection of the best 35 photos I took during this trip, composed of:
- 16 species of birds
- 2 species of mammals
- 3 species of reptiles
- 1 species of butterfly
